Complaints procedure

A clear way to be heard.

Tell us your concern. We will explain the next steps, keep you informed and use what we learn to improve.

Raise a concern↗Call +447519560119↗

1. Tell us what happened

Contact us on +447519560119, email dayspringcentre@dayspringcentre.co.uk, or write to Dayspring Centre Limited, 2 Athena Apartment, Jubilee Way, Sidcup, DA14 4BL.

Give a general outline of the issue, when it happened, the service involved and what you would like us to consider. Please contact us before sharing confidential records so that we can agree an appropriate route.

2. We acknowledge and explain the next step

Your concern will be directed to the person responsible for complaints. We will clarify the issues, explain who will review them and tell you the expected response timeframe. If you need communication support or someone to help you, please let us know.

3. We look into your concern

We review the relevant information and speak with those involved where appropriate. We may contact you to understand your experience more clearly. Information is handled with care and shared only as appropriate to the review or safeguarding obligations.

4. We explain the outcome

We will explain our findings, the reasons for the outcome and any steps to put things right or improve the service. If more time is needed, we will explain the reason and give a revised expected timeframe.

5. Ask for a further review

If you remain unhappy, contact us and explain which parts of the response you would like reviewed. Ask for the concern to be considered by an appropriate senior person. We will explain the review arrangements and any relevant external route.

Independent routes and urgent concerns

If your support was arranged by a local authority or another commissioning organisation, you can also contact that organisation about its complaints process. For concerns about registered childcare, see GOV.UK guidance on complaining about childcare. Data-protection concerns may be raised with the Information Commissioner’s Office.

Safeguarding must not wait for a complaints review.

Use the safeguarding reporting routes if someone may be at risk. If anyone is in immediate danger, call 999.

Request the full organisational policy

Contact us to request a copy of the full complaints policy and any service-specific arrangements. This page sets out the public complaints journey; any applicable statutory complaints rights remain unchanged.
