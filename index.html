A little clarity

Questions?
You’re in good company.

Here are a few things people often ask. If yours isn’t here, we’d be happy to help.

Ask us a question↗
What support is right for me or my family?

Start with a conversation. We will listen to what is working well, where you would like help and what you hope to achieve. Together, we can explore whether our services are the right fit.

Which areas do you cover?

We are based in Sidcup and support communities across England. Availability, travel and the type of support needed are discussed before any service is agreed.

Can I make a referral for someone else?

Families and professionals can contact us to discuss a referral. Please make sure you have an appropriate basis for sharing someone’s information. Start with a general enquiry; we will explain how to share detailed information safely.

Is your childcare registered with Ofsted?

Our childcare provision is registered with Ofsted under registration number EY552018, as set out in our service information. This relates to childcare provision and should not be read as a registration or rating for our other services.

How much does support cost?

Costs depend on the support needed, its frequency and location. We discuss the options and provide an individual quotation before you decide. Please call us to talk through your needs and any funding arrangements.
