The conversation planner

The optional forms on this site create a summary in your browser. They do not submit an enquiry to dayspring or store form contents in browser storage. If you choose “Open email draft”, the summary is passed to your email application so you can review and send it. Sending it shares the details with dayspring at dayspringcentre@dayspringcentre.co.uk. Any summary you choose to download is saved on your device. Keep it secure, especially on shared devices.

Do not enter health information, detailed safeguarding concerns or another person’s personal records. Call us first to agree how to share information appropriately. If you choose to use an email or telephone application, that service will handle the information you share according to its own terms.

Technical information

Vercel hosts this website and processes technical request information, such as IP addresses and browser information, to deliver and protect the site. Google Fonts supplies the typefaces; loading these fonts makes requests to Google. These providers may process data outside the UK under their applicable arrangements. See Vercel’s privacy policy and Google Fonts privacy information.

Preferences on your device

We use local storage for accessibility preferences and whether you have dismissed the cookie notice. We do not use advertising trackers or analytics cookies. You can clear these choices through your browser settings.

When you contact us directly

dayspring will need relevant contact information to respond. Before detailed referral, care or recruitment information is collected, ask for the privacy information appropriate to that service, including purposes, lawful basis, recipients and retention arrangements. This website notice does not replace those service-specific notices.

Your rights and questions

Depending on the circumstances, data protection law gives you rights to access, correct, erase or restrict use of your personal information, and to object to certain uses. Where processing is based on consent, you may withdraw it. Contact dayspring to ask about your information. You can also raise a concern with the Information Commissioner’s Office.
