Professionals & referrals

The right partnership.
Better possibilities.

A clear, collaborative referral journey for professionals, families and the people they support.

Start a referral by phone↗Explore services↗

Working together, with the person at the centre.

We welcome appropriate enquiries from local authorities, social workers, NHS professionals, schools, colleges, housing and community organisations, families and private individuals.

Our first conversation is about suitability: the person’s needs and wishes, the outcomes sought and whether DaySpring can provide safe, appropriate support.

How a referral progresses

01 · Initial enquiry

Contact us with a general outline of the support required.

02 · Initial review

We consider suitability, service availability and the next step.

03 · Assessment

Where appropriate, we arrange an assessment with the person and relevant representatives.

04 · Support planning

We agree outcomes, preferences, identified risks and support arrangements.

05 · Getting started

Support begins once the arrangements and any necessary agreements are confirmed.

06 · Ongoing review

We review how support is working and respond to changing needs.

Sharing information safely

Please call before sending personal records. We will agree an appropriate route for sharing detailed referral information, including consent or other relevant authority, communication needs, current support, risks, desired outcomes, proposed start date and funding arrangements.

Start with a general conversation.

Do not use the optional planner below to record a referred person’s name, date of birth, full address, health records or detailed risks. It is a preparation tool, not a secure referral submission.

Prepare for our conversation

Prepare a general enquiry, then open it in your email app to review and send to dayspringcentre@dayspringcentre.co.uk. This website does not send it automatically. For detailed referrals or CVs, contact us to agree a suitable route.

Please keep this general. Do not enter someone else’s name, medical information, safeguarding details, or other sensitive information.
