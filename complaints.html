Feedback & complaints

We’re listening.
Help us put things right.

Your experience matters. Feedback gives us the opportunity to understand, respond and learn.

Discuss your feedback↗Explore services↗

Your feedback helps us improve.

Please tell us if something has not met your expectations. You can raise a concern about our service, communication or support arrangements, or share a suggestion for improvement.

How to get in touch

Call 07519 560119, or write to DaySpring Centre Limited, 2 Athena Apartment, Jubilee Way, Sidcup, DA14 4BL. Ask for your concern to be directed to the person responsible for complaints.

Explain what happened, roughly when it happened and what you would like us to consider. Ask us to explain the complaints process, expected response times and any relevant escalation route.

Support to be heard

Tell us if you need a different communication format or would like someone to help you raise your concern. If you are acting for another person, we may need to clarify their wishes or your authority to act.

If your concern involves safety or possible harm, use the safeguarding routes. Call 999 in an emergency.

Prepare for our conversation

Prepare a general enquiry, then open it in your email app to review and send to dayspringcentre@dayspringcentre.co.uk. This website does not send it automatically. For detailed referrals or CVs, contact us to agree a suitable route.

Please keep this general. Do not enter someone else’s name, medical information, safeguarding details, or other sensitive information.
