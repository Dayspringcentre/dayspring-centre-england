Cookies & device storage

Cookies & device storage

A simple website with no advertising trackers.

What this site uses

This site does not set analytics or advertising cookies. It saves the following choices using local storage in your browser:

  • dayspring-notice: whether you dismissed the privacy notice.
  • dayspring-display: your larger-text and contrast preferences.

These choices stay on your device until you clear site data. Form entries are not saved to local storage. Necessary hosting or security technologies may be used by our hosting provider to deliver and protect the site.

Changing your choices

Use the Accessibility button to change display preferences. The Cookie settings link at the foot of every page reopens the notice. To remove saved choices, clear this website’s data in your browser settings.

External services

Fonts load from Google Fonts. External links only take you to another service when you choose to follow them. See our privacy information for more about these services.
