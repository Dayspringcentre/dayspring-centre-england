A place to feel heard.

DaySpring Centre Limited is a care, childcare, community support and professional consultancy organisation based in Sidcup. We welcome enquiries from individuals, families and organisations across England.

We believe good support begins by listening. It means noticing someone’s strengths, respecting their choices and understanding the life they want to live. Sometimes that means practical help with everyday routines. Sometimes it means encouragement, connection or simply time to be heard.

Our mission

To provide safe, compassionate support that helps children, young people and adults build independence, wellbeing and quality of life, while helping care organisations strengthen the quality of their services.

Our vision

To be a trusted part of the communities we serve, helping people and organisations make positive changes that last.

The values behind our work

Safety

People’s welfare and safeguarding guide our decisions.

Compassion

Kindness, patience and understanding belong in every interaction.

Dignity & choice

Your rights, privacy and decisions matter.

Inclusion

Culture, identity, beliefs, abilities and differences are respected.

Accountability

We take responsibility for our actions and communicate clearly.

Continuous improvement

We listen to feedback, reflect and look for ways to do better.

The people behind your support

Trust grows when you know who you’re working with. Before support begins, we will explain the proposed arrangements, the roles involved and who to contact with questions. Ask us about the experience and suitability of the people who would support you.

Registration & professional standards

Our childcare provision is Ofsted registered under registration number EY552018. This registration relates to childcare; it is not presented as a registration or inspection rating for adult services or consultancy.

DaySpring Centre Limited is registered in England and Wales, company number 11290144.

Services are agreed only where we can safely and appropriately meet the person’s needs, within the scope of any applicable registration and legal requirements.
