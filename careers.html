Bring your kindness. Make it count.

Good support grows from everyday actions: listening carefully, keeping your word and noticing when someone needs encouragement. We welcome interest from people who share our commitment to dignity, inclusion and dependable support.

Areas of work

  • Support workers
  • Care and community support workers
  • Children’s support workers
  • Childcare practitioners
  • Senior support staff
  • Coordinators
  • Quality and compliance professionals
Expressions of interest welcome

These are areas of work, not a list of confirmed vacancies. Contact us to ask about current opportunities, role requirements and how to apply.

What matters to us

We value people who communicate well, respect professional boundaries, take safeguarding seriously and treat others with dignity. Reliability, accountability and a willingness to learn are just as important as practical skills.

Safer recruitment

Appointments are subject to appropriate checks for the role. These may include DBS checks where required, references, identity and right-to-work verification, employment history checks and verification of qualifications and experience.

Interested in joining us?

Call to discuss opportunities and arrange an appropriate way to submit your CV. Please do not include identity documents, background-check certificates or sensitive employment details in an initial enquiry.

Prepare for our conversation

Prepare a general enquiry, then open it in your email app to review and send to dayspringcentre@dayspringcentre.co.uk. This website does not send it automatically. For detailed referrals or CVs, contact us to agree a suitable route.
