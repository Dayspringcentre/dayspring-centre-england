Adult care & support

More choice.
More everyday possibility.

Thoughtful support to help you live with dignity, independence and connection.

Enquire about adult support↗Call 07596 510845↗

Your everyday life, your way.

Having support should help you keep doing the things that make you feel like yourself. We work with you to maintain existing abilities, build confidence and make room for what you enjoy.

We start by asking what matters to you, what you already do independently and where a little help would make a difference.

Support may include

  • Companionship and emotional wellbeing
  • Everyday routines and organisation
  • Meal planning and preparation
  • Shopping and household activities
  • Attending appointments
  • Community access and social activities
  • Maintaining relationships and interests
  • Independent living skills
  • Correspondence and everyday paperwork
  • Wellbeing checks
  • Respite and family support
  • Reducing loneliness and isolation

A plan shaped with you

We discuss your preferences, strengths, communication needs and desired outcomes. Family members or relevant professionals can be involved where appropriate and with regard to your wishes.

Your support plan is reviewed as circumstances change. We want support to remain useful, respectful and responsive.

Finding the right fit

We confirm availability and suitability before agreeing support. Activities requiring specific regulatory registration are only provided where they fall within dayspring’s applicable registration and legal scope. Please contact us to discuss any personal care or specialist needs.
