Care quality & compliance consultancy

Stronger services.
More confident teams.

Practical, independent support to strengthen governance, improve quality and prepare for regulatory scrutiny.

Discuss a consultation↗Call 07519 560119↗

Quality that lives in everyday practice.

Good governance should make it easier to deliver safe, consistent care. Our consultancy helps health and social care providers understand where improvements are needed and turn findings into practical action.

We work with providers and registered managers to review systems, clarify priorities and develop evidence of improvement. The scope is agreed around your service and its needs.

Where we can help

Readiness & mock inspections

Independent reviews against applicable quality and regulatory expectations, including CQC readiness.

Quality audits

Reviews of care plans, risk assessments, MAR records, daily records, staff files and quality documentation.

Governance reviews

Systems for managing risk, incidents, complaints, safeguarding and continuous improvement.

Policies & procedures

Practical reviews and development of policies appropriate to your organisation.

Care documentation

Improvements to assessments, support plans, risk assessments and associated records.

Medication governance

MAR audits, documentation reviews, PRN governance and competency frameworks.

Workforce compliance

Recruitment records, supervision, appraisal, competency and training monitoring.

Action & improvement plans

Clear, prioritised SMART actions following audits, inspections or identified concerns.

Understand. Improve. Sustain.

Our approach moves through assessment, identifying gaps, prioritising action, making improvements, gathering evidence and monitoring progress. We explain why a finding matters and what a useful next step could look like.

DaySpring provides independent consultancy. We do not represent CQC or Ofsted, and consultancy cannot guarantee a registration decision, inspection result or rating.
