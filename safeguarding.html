Is someone in immediate danger?

Call 999 now. This website and our general enquiries route are not emergency services.

Everyone has the right to feel safe.

Dayspring Centre is committed to safeguarding children, young people and adults from abuse, neglect, exploitation and avoidable harm. Concerns should be listened to and taken seriously.

Our approach

  • Listen to people and respect their voice
  • Use appropriate safer recruitment procedures
  • Provide role-appropriate safeguarding training
  • Recognise and respond to concerns
  • Work with relevant statutory agencies
  • Keep clear and appropriate records
  • Learn from incidents and feedback
  • Share information appropriately when safeguarding requires it

Raising a concern about Dayspring Centre

Call +447519560119 to raise a concern relating to our service or a person receiving our support. If you cannot reach us, or the concern needs urgent statutory attention, contact the relevant local authority safeguarding service directly.

You may report concerns directly to statutory services without first contacting Dayspring Centre. Avoid putting confidential details into the website planner.

Independent help and reporting

GOV.UK: report child abuse explains how to contact a child’s local council social care team. For an adult safeguarding concern, contact adult social care at the relevant local council.

Find your local council to locate the appropriate service. Children and young people can also contact Childline on 0800 1111.

Privacy and safeguarding

Information is treated with care. Confidentiality cannot be promised where sharing information with an appropriate authority may be necessary to protect someone from harm.
