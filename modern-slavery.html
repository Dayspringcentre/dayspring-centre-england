Modern slavery & exploitation

Modern slavery & exploitation

Dignity, freedom and safe working.

Our position

Dayspring Centre opposes slavery, forced labour, human trafficking and exploitation. Respect for people’s rights and dignity is central to the support we seek to provide.

Raising a concern

If you suspect exploitation involving our services or working arrangements, contact Dayspring Centre on +447519560119. Where there is a risk of harm, use the appropriate statutory safeguarding route. Call 999 if someone is in immediate danger.

Further information

This page explains our public position and reporting route. It is not presented as an audited supply-chain report or a statutory annual modern slavery statement. Contact us to request relevant organisational policies.
