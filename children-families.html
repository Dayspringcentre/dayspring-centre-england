Ofsted-registered childcare provision

Registration number EY552018. Please contact us to discuss the age range, sessions and arrangements suitable for your child.

Small discoveries. Growing confidence.

Children need space to be curious, to play and to feel that they belong. We aim to create a nurturing environment where each child is recognised as an individual, and parents and carers feel heard too.

We take time to understand a child’s interests, routines, communication and support needs. That understanding helps us plan activities and care that are appropriate for them.

Safeguarding comes first

People working with children are subject to appropriate safer recruitment procedures for their roles. These may include enhanced DBS checks where required, references, identity and right-to-work checks, employment history and qualification checks, safeguarding training and ongoing supervision.
